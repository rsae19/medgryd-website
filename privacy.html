Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2026 · Last Updated: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

MedGryd ("we," "us," or "our") is a student productivity platform built for medical students. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use MedGryd, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights over your data.

By creating an account or using MedGryd, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you are using MedGryd through your medical school's license, your school may have additional data agreements with us — refer to your school's privacy notices as well.

2. Who We Are

MedGryd is an independent software product developed by Raayed Saeed and Aaron Smith (co-developer, Quiz Mastery feature). We are the data controllers for all personal information processed through the app.

📧 hello@medgryd.com — for privacy requests, use subject line "Privacy Request."

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information (via Google Sign-In)

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address, display name, profile photo URL, and a unique Firebase user ID (UID). We do not receive your Google password, payment information, or any other Google account data.

3.2 Study & Planner Data

We store the following in Firebase Firestore under your account:

  • Weekly planner tasks — titles, times, completion status, notes, links, and priority
  • Calendar events — synced from Google Calendar for your weekly view
  • Exams and goals — names, dates, and personal study goals you enter
  • Study session time — cumulative hours tracked by date
  • Anki flashcard counts — daily review counts only (no card content)
  • Skills Vault — confidence ratings and notes for OMM techniques and clinical skills checklists
  • Score Matrix — assessment scores and grade projections you enter
  • OSCE Tracker — notes and scores from OSCE practice sessions
  • Quick Jot — rich text notes organized in sections
  • App settings and preferences — theme, colors, calendar configurations, and other UI preferences
  • School and cohort — whether you attend a partner school and which class year/cohort, used to show you the right exams, passwords, and leaderboard

3.3 Quiz Mastery Data

If you use the Quiz Mastery feature: uploaded quiz sets (questions, answer choices, explanations), your answers, confidence ratings, attempt history, and per-topic mastery scores.

3.4 Research Hub Data

Personal research projects (title, status, co-authors, journal, links, task lists, deadlines). Shared projects are stored in a shared collection accessible only to members you invite via invite code.

3.5 GrydRank / Leaderboard Data

XP, level, badges, and aggregate study/task/Anki totals. Leaderboard participation is opt-in. If you opt in, your display name, profile photo, and stats are visible to cohort members. You may enable anonymous mode to display as "Anonymous." Study group members can see your stats within the group.

3.6 Peer Board

Posts and replies carry no name and no user ID — nothing stored on a post links it back to your account. The only record that a post is yours lives in your own account data, on your side. This means posts are genuinely anonymous to other students and to us: we can remove a post, but we cannot tell who wrote it.

3.7 Presence Information

When active, your online/offline status, display name, and last-seen timestamp are written to Firebase Realtime Database for group features.

3.8 Activation Data

Your activation code, display name, and Firebase UID are sent to our activation validation service (a Google Apps Script) to verify and claim your code. MedGryd itself does not retain this data after validation; the validation service keeps a record of which code was claimed so a code cannot be reused.

3.9 Quiz Password Cache (Partner Schools)

Where a school distributes quiz passwords to its cohort, MedGryd fetches and caches them in a shared Firestore document visible to students in that same cohort, as operational data for coursework.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service — display your planner, sync calendar events, track study progress
  • Personalize your experience — apply your settings, theme, and preferences
  • Power social/collaborative features — leaderboard, peer board, study groups, shared research
  • Improve the app — diagnose errors and bugs using error reports
  • Enforce our terms — detect abuse of the peer board or activation system
  • Understand feature usage — we look at whether and how often each feature is used, both per account and across the cohort, to decide what to build and fix. This shows us usage counts and frequency only — never the contents of your notes, grades, tasks, or scores
  • License to medical schools — aggregate non-identifiable usage patterns may be shared with licensed institutions

We do not use your data for advertising, sell your data to third parties, or use it for purposes unrelated to providing MedGryd.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google / Firebase

Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Realtime Database, and Storage are used as our backend. Data is stored in the United States. Google Privacy Policy →

5.2 Google Calendar & Tasks API

We access your Google Calendar (read and write) to sync events into your planner and to push events you create or edit in MedGryd back to your Google Calendar. We also request read and write access to Google Tasks, used to read your task lists and update tasks you manage through MedGryd. Calendar and Tasks data are not shared with any party other than Google and Firebase.

5.3 Sentry (Error Monitoring & Session Replay)

We use Sentry for error tracking. Sentry may collect JavaScript errors, network request metadata, browser information, and — because we send default personal data — your IP address and account identifiers. Approximately 10% of sessions are recorded as interactive replays; 100% of sessions in which an error occurs are recorded. Our current configuration masks neither on-screen text nor images, so content you enter (task names, notes) and anything visible on screen may appear in error reports and replays visible to our development team. We are evaluating stricter masking before broader institutional deployment. Sentry Privacy Policy →

5.4 Tawk.to (Support Chat)

If you use the in-app support chat, your conversation and session metadata are processed by Tawk.to. Tawk.to Privacy Policy →

5.5 Vercel (Hosting)

MedGryd is hosted on Vercel, which may log standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamps). Vercel Privacy Policy →

5.6 AnkiConnect (Local, Optional)

Anki integration communicates only with AnkiConnect running on your own computer (localhost). No Anki data is sent to external servers — only daily review counts are stored in MedGryd.

5.7 Canvas (via Google Calendar)

MedGryd does not connect to Canvas directly and has no Canvas login or API access. Your Canvas assignments reach MedGryd only through the Canvas calendar feed you subscribe to in your own Google Calendar, and arrive as ordinary calendar events.

5.8 AI Question Generation (Quiz Mastery)

Quiz Mastery’s question generation runs in the AI tool you choose — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another. MedGryd gives you a prompt to paste in yourself and imports the .json file the tool returns. MedGryd never sends your lecture material, notes, or any other data to an AI provider. Whatever you paste into that tool is governed by that tool’s own privacy policy and your school’s academic policies.

6. Educational Records & FERPA

If you use MedGryd through a medical school with a data agreement, certain data (grades, study time, self-assessed competency) may constitute educational records under FERPA. In such cases, we act as a school official with a legitimate educational interest and do not disclose records without consent except as permitted by FERPA.

Currently (beta period): MedGryd is used independently — no educational record sharing occurs.

7. Data Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption in transit)
  • Firebase Firestore uses Google's infrastructure (data encrypted at rest)
  • Firestore Security Rules tie your data to your Firebase UID — no other student can read or write it
  • The developer can access account data for the usage review described in Section 4, and for support and debugging when you ask for help
  • Peer Board posts are stored in one shared document that any signed-in MedGryd user can read — it is a public anonymous board by design, so treat anything you post there as public
  • Only the development team has access to Sentry error reports and session replays
  • We maintain nightly encrypted database backups for disaster recovery, retained for up to 30 days

No internet service is 100% secure. We recommend a strong Google account password and two-factor authentication.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: View your data in the app; request a machine-readable export by email.
  • Correction: Edit or delete tasks, notes, goals, etc. at any time through the app.
  • Deletion: Email hello@medgryd.com with subject "Account Deletion Request." We will delete your Firestore documents within 30 days. Your data may also persist in an encrypted backup for up to 30 days after that before it is purged in the normal backup rotation cycle. Peer Board posts are never linked to your account, so they are already anonymous and cannot be found and removed for you after the fact — delete any you no longer want from the Peer Board yourself while still signed in.
  • Opt-out of Leaderboard: Disable participation or enable anonymous mode in Settings → GrydRank.
  • Opt-out of Sentry replay: No per-user opt-out currently available. Contact us if this is a concern.

9. Data Retention

  • Personal data is retained while your account is active. Inactive accounts may be purged after 24 months.
  • Leaderboard entries are removed when you delete your account.
  • Peer Board posts are soft-deleted (body cleared) when you delete them from the board yourself; the post shell may remain.
  • Sentry error reports are retained per Sentry's standard retention period (90 days for session replays).
  • Activation validation data is not retained by MedGryd after validation.
  • Nightly database backups are retained on a rolling 30-day basis, then automatically purged.

10. Children's Privacy

MedGryd is designed exclusively for medical students (adults aged 18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Contact us if you believe a minor has created an account.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app. Continued use after the notice constitutes acceptance.
