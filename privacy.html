Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2026 · Last Updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

MedGryd is a student productivity platform built for medical students, operated by MedGryd LLC ("MedGryd," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use MedGryd, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights over your data.

By creating an account or using MedGryd, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you are using MedGryd through your medical school's license, your school may have additional data agreements with us — refer to your school's privacy notices as well.

Some features are optional and send data to outside services only when you use them. Section 5 lists every one of those services and exactly what each receives.

2. Who We Are

MedGryd is owned and operated by MedGryd LLC, founded by Raayed Saeed and Aaron Smith. MedGryd LLC is the data controller for all personal information processed through the app.

📧 hello@medgryd.com — for privacy requests, use subject line "Privacy Request."

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Account Information (Google, Apple or Email Sign-In)

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address, display name, profile photo URL, and a unique Firebase user ID (UID). We do not receive your Google password or any other Google account data. Your email address is saved to your account so our servers can send the emails described in Section 3.13.

You can also sign in with Apple, and add Apple or Google as a second sign-in method for the same account in Settings → Account. With Apple we receive the email address Apple shares with us (which may be a private relay address that forwards to you), your name if you choose to share it, and a Firebase UID. We do not receive your Apple ID password.

You can also sign in with an email address and password, or add a password to an account you made with Google or Apple. With email sign-in we receive the email address and the name you enter, and a Firebase UID. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google), which keeps only a salted hash of it; MedGryd never sees or stores your password. We send one email to verify the address, and a password-reset email only when you ask for one.

3.2 Study & Planner Data

We store the following in Firebase Firestore under your account:

  • Weekly planner tasks — titles, times, completion status, notes, links, priority, and subtasks
  • Calendar events — synced from Google Calendar and from your school's LMS calendar feed
  • Exams and goals — names, dates, and personal study goals you enter
  • Study session (Focus) time — time tracked by date and by session, including time spent in Quiz Mastery and CaseFlow
  • Anki counts — daily review counts, and (if you use Board Ops) counts of cards by deck or tag. Never card content
  • Skills Vault — confidence ratings and notes for OMM techniques and clinical skills checklists
  • Score Matrix — assessment scores and grade projections you enter
  • OSCE Tracker — notes and scores from practice sessions
  • Quick Jot — rich text notes organized in sections, including notes you pin to the screen
  • Board Ops — board exam dates, your study capacity, question-bank names and totals you report, logged question blocks (counts, accuracy, notes on misses), your course schedule, and practice-exam scores
  • App settings and preferences — theme, colors, calendar configurations, and other UI preferences
  • School and cohort — whether you attend a partner school and which class year/cohort, used to show you the right exams, passwords, and leaderboard

3.3 Quiz Mastery Data

Quiz sets you import or generate (questions, answer choices, explanations), source coverage maps, your answers, confidence ratings, attempt history, per-topic mastery scores, saved GrydGuide study guides, the follow-up questions you type in Deeper explanation about questions you got wrong (used to tailor your GrydGuide; the AI's replies are not saved), your GrydAI conversations (your messages and GrydAI's replies) and short notes of what you asked about the Quiz Mastery branches and concepts a conversation was linked to (also used to tailor your GrydGuide), and a weekly count of the AI generation tokens you have used.

3.4 CaseFlow Data

For the simulated patient cases you run: your conversation with the simulated patient, the exams and tests you order, your SOAP note, your oral-presentation transcript, timing, and the scores and feedback you receive. We keep full records of your 25 most recent cases; older cases keep only a summary.

Using the microphone is optional. If you record a presentation, the audio is sent for transcription (Section 5.8) and is not stored by MedGryd — only the text transcript is saved. If you use your browser's built-in dictation, your browser provider (such as Google or Apple) processes that audio under its own privacy policy.

3.5 Research Hub Data

Personal research projects (title, status, co-authors, journal, links, task lists, deadlines). Shared projects can be read only by the project's owner, its members, and people with a pending invitation. If you import literature from a Google Sheets link, MedGryd reads that sheet through Google and adds the rows you confirm.

3.6 GrydRank, Profiles & Study Groups

XP, level, badges, streak, activity totals (study time, tasks, Anki cards, quiz questions, CaseFlow cases), daily activity for roughly the last 40 days, average Productivity Score, and — if you choose to add them — a short bio and up to three specialty interests.

  • Cohort leaderboard (opt-in). If you opt in, signed-in students can see your display name, photo, stats, and profile card (bio, specialty interests, last-30-day totals, personal bests, and recent daily activity). Anonymous mode shows you as "Anonymous" and hides your name, photo, bio, and specialty interests.
  • Study groups. If you join a group, its members can see your display name, photo, and the same stats and profile — whether or not you opted into the cohort leaderboard, and even if you use anonymous mode there. Leave a group to stop sharing with it.

3.7 Sharing & Invitations

When you share a calendar event, an On My Radar goal, a research project, or a Quiz Mastery course, or invite someone to a study group, the people you invite see your display name and the shared item. Members of a shared Quiz Mastery course can see your display name and the question sets you add to it. An invitation includes your display name and the item's name, and may also be emailed to the person you invited (Section 3.13).

3.8 Peer Board

Posts show no name or account information to other students. So that you — and only you — can delete your own post, we keep a private record of which account wrote each post, stored separately from the post. Other users cannot read it. Our development team can access it through our database administration tools, and does so only to investigate a serious violation of our Terms or when required by law.

Replies and upvotes are recorded on the post with your account ID (never shown in the app, but readable by other signed-in users who inspect the app's data). Replies and upvotes are therefore not fully anonymous. Treat anything you post on the Peer Board as public.

3.9 Presence Information

When active, your online/offline status, display name, and last-seen timestamp are written to Firebase Realtime Database for group features.

3.10 Activation & Plan Records

Your activation code, display name, email address, and Firebase UID are sent to our activation service (a Google Apps Script backed by a Google Sheet that only our team can access). The sheet records which account claimed each code, with that account's email and plan, so a code cannot be reused and your plan can be applied.

3.11 Payments

If you buy a paid plan, checkout is handled by Stripe. Where shown at checkout, Link (a Stripe service) sells the plan as reseller and merchant of record. Stripe and Link collect your payment details, billing email, and the location information needed for tax, and process them under their own privacy policies. MedGryd never sees or stores your card number. We store a Stripe customer ID, your plan, billing period, subscription status, and renewal date.

3.12 Quiz Password Cache (Partner Schools)

Where a school distributes quiz passwords to its cohort, MedGryd fetches and caches them in a shared Firestore document visible to students in that same cohort, as operational data for coursework.

3.13 Email

We use your email address to send (a) notifications when someone shares something with you or invites you, and (b) a weekly study summary on Sunday evenings. Both are on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Account. We may also email you about your account or a paid plan; payment receipts come from Stripe or Link.

3.14 Browser Storage

MedGryd keeps some data in your browser's local storage — preferences, cached AI explanations, an in-progress CaseFlow case, and XP waiting to be saved — so the app loads quickly and survives a reload. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. The support chat (Tawk.to) and error monitoring (Sentry) may set their own storage, as described in Section 5. The MedGryd phone apps keep the same kinds of data in the app's own storage on your device.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide the service — display your planner, sync calendar events, track study progress
  • Personalize your experience — apply your settings, theme, and preferences
  • Power social/collaborative features — leaderboard, profiles, peer board, study groups, shared courses, events, goals, and research
  • Run AI features you use — send the specific content listed in Section 5.8 to generate questions, explanations, study guides, and simulated patient cases
  • Process payments — apply the plan you bought and keep it in sync with Stripe
  • Send email — invitations and the weekly summary, unless you turn them off
  • Improve the app — diagnose errors and bugs using error reports
  • Enforce our terms — detect abuse of the peer board, AI usage limits, or the activation system
  • Understand feature usage — we look at whether and how often each feature is used, both per account and across the cohort, to decide what to build and fix. This shows us usage counts and frequency only — never the contents of your notes, grades, tasks, or scores
  • License to medical schools — aggregate non-identifiable usage patterns may be shared with licensed institutions

We do not use your data for advertising, sell your data to third parties, or use it for purposes unrelated to providing MedGryd.

5. Third-Party Services

5.1 Google / Firebase

Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Realtime Database are used as our backend. Data is stored in the United States. Google Privacy Policy →

5.2 Google Calendar & Tasks API

We access your Google Calendar (read and write) to sync events into your planner and to push events you create or edit in MedGryd back to your Google Calendar. We also request read and write access to Google Tasks, used to read your task lists and update tasks you manage through MedGryd. Calendar and Tasks data are not shared with any party other than Google and Firebase.

When you connect Google Calendar, Google issues MedGryd a long-lived authorization (a refresh token) so you stay connected on every device without signing in to Google again. We store it encrypted (AES-256) on our servers, where no app or browser can read it, and use it only to get short-lived access to your own calendar and tasks when MedGryd syncs. Disconnecting in Settings → Account, or deleting your account, deletes it and revokes it at Google. You can also revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5.3 Sentry (Error Monitoring & Session Replay)

We use Sentry for error tracking. Sentry may collect JavaScript errors, network request metadata, browser information, and — because we send default personal data — your IP address and account identifiers. Approximately 10% of sessions are recorded as interactive replays; 100% of sessions in which an error occurs are recorded. Our current configuration masks neither on-screen text nor images, so content you enter (task names, notes) and anything visible on screen — including AI explanations, study guides, and CaseFlow conversations — may appear in error reports and replays visible to our development team. We are evaluating stricter masking before broader institutional deployment. Sentry Privacy Policy →

5.4 Tawk.to (Support Chat)

If you use the support chat, your conversation and session metadata are processed by Tawk.to. Tawk.to Privacy Policy →

5.5 Vercel (Hosting)

MedGryd is hosted on Vercel, which also runs our server functions — the ones that pass requests to the AI, email, and payment services below. Vercel may log standard request metadata (IP address, user agent, timestamps). Vercel Privacy Policy →

5.6 AnkiConnect (Local, Optional)

Anki integration talks only to AnkiConnect on your own computer (localhost). No Anki data is sent to external servers — only review counts are stored in MedGryd.

5.7 Your School's LMS (via Google Calendar)

MedGryd does not connect to Canvas, D2L Brightspace, or any other learning management system directly, and has no LMS login or API access. Your assignments reach MedGryd only through the LMS calendar feed you subscribe to in your own Google Calendar, and arrive as ordinary calendar events.

5.8 AI Features

Several optional features send content to AI providers through our servers. They run only when you use the feature, and only the content listed below is sent — never your name or email address. Each provider processes that content under its own terms and privacy policy.

  • Quiz Mastery question generation → Anthropic (Claude): the source material you provide (such as lecture text) and its coverage map. The questions it writes are saved to your account. Anthropic Privacy Policy →
  • Writing a set with your own AI (Quiz Mastery, paid plans) → you choose an AI service (such as Claude or ChatGPT) and connect it to MedGryd. Your notes stay with that service, under its own terms. MedGryd sends that service its question-writing instructions, and the service sends MedGryd a coverage map of your notes (topic names, short descriptions and where each topic is in the notes) and the questions it writes, which MedGryd checks and saves to your account. For a set written from your difficult Anki cards, the cards are the source: MedGryd sends the text of the selected cards (their front, back and fields, deck name, tags, and how often you missed them) to that service through the connector.
  • Deeper explanation (Quiz Mastery) → Google (Gemini), with Groq as a backup: the question, its answer choices, the keyed answer, and the stored explanation; for follow-up questions, also the explanation you were shown, the answer you chose, and the conversation so far.
  • GrydAI (paid plans) → Google (Gemini), with Groq as a backup: your message, the last few turns of the conversation and a short summary of earlier ones, the names of up to 20 of your Quiz Mastery courses, branches and concepts that match what you asked, and the name of the page you are on.
  • GrydGuide study guides → Google (Gemini), with Groq as a backup: the concepts you are being asked to relearn, up to four questions you missed for each (with the answer you chose, the stored explanation, and any follow-up questions you asked about it in Deeper explanation), what you asked GrydAI about those concepts or that branch, and topic names and descriptions from your source coverage map.
  • CaseFlow → Google (Gemini), with Groq as a backup: your case choices (level, course or topic, and the names of any weak concepts or skills you pick to focus on), your conversation with the simulated patient, and — for grading — your SOAP note and presentation transcript. Presentation recordings → Groq (Whisper) for transcription. The simulated patient's replies (text written by the AI, not by you) → Microsoft Azure AI Speech, to produce the patient's voice.

Google Privacy Policy → · Groq Privacy Policy → · Microsoft Privacy Statement →

Where MedGryd instead gives you a prompt to paste into an AI tool you choose (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or another), whatever you paste there is governed by that tool's own privacy policy and your school's academic policies. Never enter real patient information into any MedGryd feature.

5.9 Stripe & Link (Payments)

Paid plans are purchased through Stripe Checkout and managed in Stripe's billing portal, as described in Section 3.11. Stripe Privacy Policy → · Link Privacy Policy →

5.10 Resend (Email Delivery)

Emails described in Section 3.13 are delivered by Resend, which receives your email address and the message content (for example, the sender's display name and the shared item's name, or your weekly study totals). Resend Privacy Policy →

5.11 Apple & Google App Stores and Phone Notifications

If you use the MedGryd app from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google distributes it and may collect download, purchase and crash information under its own privacy policy. Sign in with Apple is processed by Apple. Reminders on this phone are scheduled by your phone's operating system: event titles and times stay on your device and are not sent to MedGryd or to any push-notification service. The apps do not sell plans or token packs; those are bought on the MedGryd website.

6. Educational Records & FERPA

If you use MedGryd through a medical school with a data agreement, certain data (grades, study time, self-assessed competency) may constitute educational records under FERPA. In such cases, we act as a school official with a legitimate educational interest and do not disclose records without consent except as permitted by FERPA.

Currently (beta period): MedGryd is used independently — no educational record sharing occurs.

7. Data Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption in transit)
  • Firebase Firestore uses Google's infrastructure (data encrypted at rest)
  • Firestore Security Rules tie your personal data to your Firebase UID — no other student can read or write it
  • Shared items (Quiz Mastery courses, calendar events, goals, research projects, study groups) can be read only by their owner, members, and people with a pending invitation
  • The leaderboard (if you opt in) and Peer Board posts can be read by any signed-in MedGryd user — treat anything you post on the Peer Board as public
  • The developer can access account data for the usage review described in Section 4, for support and debugging when you ask for help, and as described in Section 3.8
  • Only the development team has access to Sentry error reports and replays
  • We maintain nightly encrypted database backups for disaster recovery, retained for up to 30 days

No internet service is 100% secure. We recommend a strong Google account password and two-factor authentication.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access: View your data in the app; request a machine-readable export by email.
  • Correction: Edit or delete tasks, notes, goals, etc. at any time through the app.
  • Deletion: Delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. This immediately deletes your MedGryd data, cancels any paid plan so it won't renew, revokes MedGryd's access to Google, and deletes your Peer Board posts along with your replies and upvotes. Shared research projects and Quiz Mastery courses you own pass to another member; shared calendar events and goals you own are deleted for everyone. You can also email hello@medgryd.com with subject "Account Deletion Request," and we will do the same within 30 days. Either way, your data may persist in an encrypted backup for up to 30 days before it is purged in the normal backup rotation cycle. Stripe and Link keep the transaction records they are legally required to keep.
  • Opt-out of Leaderboard: Disable participation or enable anonymous mode in Settings → GrydRank. Leave a study group to stop sharing your stats with it.
  • Email: Turn invitation emails and the weekly summary on or off in Settings → Account.
  • Notifications: In the phone app, Reminders on this phone stay off until you turn them on in Settings → Account. Turn them off there or in your phone's settings.
  • Microphone: Optional, used only in CaseFlow when you press record. You can revoke it in your browser's settings at any time.
  • Opt-out of Sentry replay: No per-user opt-out currently available. Contact us if this is a concern.

9. Data Retention

  • Personal data is retained while your account is active. Inactive accounts may be purged after 24 months.
  • Leaderboard entries and study-group stats are removed when you delete your account.
  • Peer Board posts are deleted when you delete them from the board yourself; replies you made to someone else's post stay until that post is deleted, unless you ask us to remove them.
  • CaseFlow keeps full records of your 25 most recent cases and a summary of older ones. Presentation audio is not retained by MedGryd.
  • GrydGuide keeps your newest 3 study guides per course branch, up to 15 in total.
  • Deeper explanation keeps your follow-up questions for your 200 most recent missed questions (up to 3 per question).
  • GrydAI keeps your 30 most recent conversations (up to 40 messages each) and its notes for up to 200 branches and concepts. You can delete a conversation in GrydAI at any time.
  • MedGryd does not keep a copy of AI requests beyond the results saved to your account and the follow-up questions described above. Our servers may briefly hold an explanation in memory so the same question asked again doesn't trigger a new request. AI providers retain request data according to their own policies.
  • Plan and subscription records are kept while your account exists, and afterward only as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations. Stripe and Link retain payment records under their own policies.
  • Activation and plan records in our activation sheet are kept while your account is active.
  • Resend keeps email delivery logs according to its own retention period.
  • Sentry error reports are retained per Sentry's standard retention period (90 days for replays).
  • Nightly database backups are retained on a rolling 30-day basis, then automatically purged.

10. Children's Privacy

MedGryd is designed exclusively for medical students (adults aged 18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Contact us if you believe a minor has created an account.
