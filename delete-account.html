Delete Your MedGryd Account

How to delete your MedGryd account and the data in it, in the app or by email, and exactly what is deleted or kept.

MedGryd is operated by MedGryd LLC. These steps apply to the MedGryd website, the installed web app, and the MedGryd apps on Google Play and the App Store: they all use the same account.

1. Delete It in the App

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open MedGryd (the app, or app.medgryd.com) and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings: on a phone, tap More in the bottom bar, then Settings; on a computer, open the ☰ Menu, then Settings.
  3. Go to the Account tab and scroll to the bottom.
  4. Choose Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, and confirm again.

Your account and its data are deleted right away and you are signed out. This can't be undone.

2. Ask Us to Delete It by Email

If you can't sign in, or don't have the app installed, email us instead.

📧 Email hello@medgryd.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request", from the email address on your MedGryd account.

We delete the account and its data in the same way as the in-app option, within 30 days, and reply to confirm. If you write from a different address, we will first ask you to show that the account is yours, so nobody can delete someone else's account.

3. What Is Deleted

  • Your sign-in (Google, Apple or email and password) and your account profile.
  • Everything you have saved in MedGryd: planner tasks, events, goals and notes, Quick Jot, templates and settings; Quiz Mastery sets, progress, reviews and GrydGuide study guides; CaseFlow cases; Skills Vault, OSCE, Board Ops, Research Hub and Score Matrix data; Study Mode time and productivity history.
  • Your GrydRank XP, stats, badges and leaderboard entry, and your membership in study groups.
  • Your Peer Board posts, and the replies and upvotes you left on other people's posts.
  • Your GrydAI conversations and notes, and your AI usage and token records.
  • The connection to your Google Calendar. MedGryd's access is revoked at Google.
  • Any paid plan is cancelled so it won't renew, and MedGryd's links to your billing records are removed.
  • The phones registered to receive your notifications, and your pending invites and online status.

4. What Happens to Things You Shared

  • Research projects and Quiz Mastery courses you own pass to another member, so your classmates keep them. If nobody else is in one, it is deleted.
  • Study groups you created pass to another member, or are deleted if you were the only member.
  • Shared calendar events and goals you own are deleted for everyone.
  • Things you added to someone else's shared course or project stay with it for its members.

5. What Is Kept, and for How Long

  • Backups: your data may remain in our encrypted nightly backups for up to 30 days, after which it is purged in the normal backup rotation.
  • Payments: Stripe and Link keep the transaction records they are legally required to keep. We keep plan and subscription records only as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations.
  • Service logs: Sentry error reports and Resend email delivery logs are kept for those services' standard retention periods.
  • App stores: Google Play and Apple keep download and purchase information under their own privacy policies.

6. Delete Some Data Without Deleting Your Account

You can delete much of your data yourself while keeping your account:

  • Delete individual tasks, events, goals, notes and Quiz Mastery sets wherever they appear.
  • Delete GrydAI conversations from GrydAI, and your own Peer Board posts from the board.
  • Disconnect Google Calendar in Settings → Account, which deletes MedGryd's stored access to it.
  • Turn off Reminders on this phone in Settings → Account, which deletes that phone's notification registration.

To have anything else deleted, email hello@medgryd.com with the subject "Privacy Request" and tell us what you want removed.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
