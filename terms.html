3. Description of Service

MedGryd is a productivity and study management platform for medical students, including: weekly planner and calendar integration, exam and goal tracking, grade calculator (Score Matrix), Skills Vault (OMM and clinical skills), research project management, Quiz Mastery (including AI question generation, AI explanations, and GrydGuide study guides), Board Ops (board-exam planning), CaseFlow (simulated patient encounters), GrydRank gamification, Peer Board, and study resource tracking.
