— The tour

Don't read about it. Click it.

Every panel below is a working miniature of the real app — same colours, same rules, same behaviour. Tick the tasks, answer the question, interview the patient.

01 — The week

A planner that already has your week in it.

Canvas or Brightspace assignments, Google Calendar events and quiz passwords land on their own. Subtasks count as real units of work, so ten Anki decks score as ten — not one.

  • Two-way Google Calendar — edit here, it changes there.
  • Subtask groups with done-out-of-total counts and live progress.
  • Quiz reviews and board blocks place themselves on the right day.
  • A personal Productivity Score measured against your own average.

Tick a task — or open the Anki group

02 — Quiz Mastery

It knows the difference between knowing and guessing.

Upload your slides and MedGryd writes the questions — board-style vignettes by Claude Opus 5.5, course-style by Sonnet 5.5 — constrained to what your lecture actually taught. Then it schedules each one on your planner.

  • Confidence on every answer — a lucky guess is not a mastered concept.
  • Choice-by-choice teaching: why not here, and when it would be right.
  • Exam Mode with flags, a scratchpad, highlights and a timer.
  • GrydGuide turns your misses into a branded PDF study guide.

Pick an answer, say how sure you are, check it

03 — CaseFlow

A patient who answers back.

An AI patient with a hidden diagnosis and a hidden worry. Take the history, examine with 311 real maneuvers, order from 59 tests, write the SOAP note, present the case out loud — then get graded on what you gathered.

  • Voice in, voice out — speak to the patient, hear them answer.
  • Exact history coverage: every fact you uncovered is tracked.
  • Oral presentation scoring for pace, fillers and structure.
  • Targets your weak spots from Quiz Mastery and Skills Vault.

Ask the patient a question

04 — Board Ops

Board prep for the pass/fail era.

COMLEX Level 1 and Step 1 are pass/fail, so Board Ops plans for margin, not throughput. It reads your QBank blocks, school exams, Anki and Quiz Mastery — each on its own scale — and builds today from what the evidence says.

  • A comfort margin you choose, not the bare passing line.
  • Content vs. process: a miss tally no QBank can give you.
  • Day lanes matched to what class is teaching this week.
  • Full-length simulations with a stamina read, block by block.

Switch the phase, hover a system

05 — Skills Vault & Score Matrix

The protocols are written. The math is done.

227 OMM techniques with full step-by-step protocols and 349 physical-exam tasks — spin the wheel when you don't know what to practise. And the grade calculator that knows which quiz gets dropped.

  • Confidence 0–5, notes and a practice timer on every technique.
  • Print any selection as a folded, saddle-stitched booklet.
  • “What do I need on the final?” for every letter grade.

Spin, then drag the midterm

06 — GrydRank

Week nine of a hard block needs momentum.

The work you were doing anyway turns into XP, levels and badges. Opt into your cohort board, or keep it to a private group of friends who'll notice when you go quiet.

  • 100 named levels across ten tiers, and 51 badges.
  • Profiles with personal bests and a 30-day record.
  • Private groups, weekly challenges and streaks.

Earn some XP
