Features

Everything in orbit.

One account, one set of data, and every tool reading from it. The ones marked New shipped this term.

Launch MedGryd → Try the live demos
01

Plan the week

The planner is the front door. Everything else hangs off it.

Weekly Planner

Core

A seven-day grid built for a schedule that changes. Drag tasks between days or whole weeks, and watch anything overdue age from yellow to red.

  • Three-state tasks: done, in progress, written off
  • Priority and a “Good Enough” mode for heavy weeks
  • Daily, weekly, block, monthly and semester views

Subtasks

New
Units of work

Ten Anki decks under one task score as ten units, not one. Drag a task onto another to nest it; rows sharing a lecture number group themselves.

  • Done-out-of-total counts and a progress bar
  • Each subtask has its own notes and three states
  • Plan Completion scores the real work

Calendar & LMS sync

Automatic

Canvas, D2L Brightspace or any iCal feed lands on its own. Google Calendar is properly two-way — edit an event here and the real one updates.

  • Recurring events with This / Following / All edits
  • Import a PDF schedule straight into the grid
  • Automatic prep and travel time before events

Today's To-Do

New
Everywhere

Pin today's list to the corner of every page and work through it without going back to the planner. Drag it anywhere on screen.

  • Mirrors the real day card
  • Tick and reorder in place
  • Stacks with the Focus timer and reminders

Quick Jot

Stickies
Capture

A rich-text scratchpad with named sections. Pin any section out as a resizable sticky note that follows you across the app.

  • Unlimited named sections
  • Checklists, headings and lists
  • Draggable, resizable sticky notes

Focus Mode

Deep work

Timed and Pomodoro sessions that log themselves into your stats, your Productivity Score and your rank — each round banked as it finishes.

  • Sessions drawn where they ran in Block view
  • Next-event countdown with a “leave in” warning
  • Hands a finished QBank block to Board Ops

Shared events & goals

Together

Invite classmates to an event or an On My Radar goal. Everyone keeps their own copy in their own calendar, and the owner's edits follow.

  • See who accepted, declined or is pending
  • Recurring shared goals stay linked
  • Works for events and goals alike

Quiz passwords

For your cohort

Where a school supports it, daily and weekly quiz passwords arrive on the task itself, with one tap to copy.

  • Refreshes through the day until it arrives
  • Copy button right on the task
  • Set up once per cohort
02

Study and practice

The part that actually takes the hours.

Quiz Mastery

Learning science

A spaced-retrieval engine with opinions. Every answer carries your confidence, so a lucky guess is never mistaken for a mastered concept.

  • Intervals stretch 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 → 60 → 120 days
  • Confidently-wrong answers flagged on their own
  • Reviews placed on your planner, on the right day

Questions from your slides

New
In-app AI

Attach a lecture and MedGryd maps what it genuinely teaches, then writes questions constrained to that material — board-style by Claude Opus 5.5, course-style by Claude Sonnet 5.5.

  • Source Coverage map, rated by yield
  • Lecture, Anki deck, or both
  • NBME-style vignettes with reference ranges inline

Exam Mode

New
Test conditions

Free navigation, revisable answers, flags, highlights, a per-question scratchpad and an optional timer — then a full review before anything is recycled.

  • Refuses to submit with blanks left
  • Replays your highlights and notes in review
  • Timer pauses on the review screen

Choice-by-choice teaching

New
Deeper explanation

GrydAI explains why the right answer is right, why each other choice isn't, and when it would be. Ask follow-up questions in the same review. Flags an answer key that looks wrong.

  • Every alternative explained
  • “Would be correct if…” for each
  • Cached, so reopening is instant

GrydAI

New
Connected study tutor · Plus & Max

Ask a study question from across the app. GrydAI links the conversation to your Quiz Mastery branches and concepts, so your next GrydGuide can address what you found confusing.

  • Suggested questions from your weak concepts
  • Saved chats and topic links you can remove
  • Pauses during quizzes and CaseFlow encounters

Try the GrydAI example →

GrydGuide

New
AI study guide

Turns your weak concepts and actual misses into a study guide with self-checks. Linked GrydAI conversations add what you asked and found confusing when you generate your next guide — exportable as a MedGryd-branded PDF.

  • “What your misses suggest” for each concept
  • Tied to your lecture's topics and slides
  • One guide per branch, refreshed when you want

Shared courses

Study together

Share a whole Quiz Mastery course with classmates. New sets reach everyone; your progress and your schedule stay yours.

  • Owner controls the structure
  • Per-person start dates and planner rows
  • Hide a set without deleting it for others

Anki → concept lessons

Nobody else does this

Reads which cards you keep failing, groups them by concept rather than deck, and builds a lesson packet from them. Live card counts feed your stats.

  • Finds weak cards by review history
  • Groups and ranks by difficulty
  • Feeds Board Ops retention by system

Companion Tracker

Resources

Pathoma chapters, SketchyPharm and SketchyMicro videos ticked off as you go — and Board Ops assigns the next ones for you.

  • Per-series progress
  • Never re-assigns what you've watched
  • Earns XP
03

Clinical skills

From the first physical exam to the OSCE room.

CaseFlow

New
Patient simulator

An AI patient with a hidden diagnosis and a hidden worry. Take the history, examine, order tests, write the SOAP note and present the case — then get graded.

  • 311 exam maneuvers, 59 orderable tests
  • Voice in and out, with transcribed presentations
  • Scored on data gathering, diagnosis, note and delivery

Skills Vault

OMM & physical exam

227 OMM techniques, every one with its full written protocol, and 349 physical-exam tasks across 18 checklists.

  • Confidence 0–5 and notes on everything
  • Practice stopwatch and picker wheel
  • Print a selection as a saddle-stitched booklet

OSCE Tracker

SOAP notes

A complete SOAP note: 18 review-of-systems categories, vitals, seven exam areas and 22 labs that flag themselves when out of range.

  • CBC, BMP and CMP with reference ranges
  • Printable patient card
  • The same note CaseFlow uses
04

Board prep

COMLEX Level 1 and USMLE Step 1, planned for the pass/fail era.

Board Ops

New
The plan

A day-by-day plan built from your real capacity and your own evidence, aimed at a comfort margin you choose — not the bare passing line.

  • Alongside School, Dedicated and Bridge Week phases
  • Day lanes matched to what class is teaching
  • Today's rows reach your planner automatically

Evidence ledger

New
Signals

QBank blocks, school exams, Score Matrix, Anki retention and Quiz Mastery — each judged on its own scale, never blended into one invented number.

  • “Not taught yet” kept apart from “weak”
  • Blueprint floors flagged for COMLEX
  • A content-vs-process miss tally

Readiness & taper

New
Go / no-go

Assessment cadence, a go/no-go call before the reschedule cutoff, and a taper that cuts volume while keeping contact.

  • Proposes; never applies itself
  • Repair options when you fall behind
  • Registration and seat-booking tracker

Full-length simulations

New
Stamina

Rehearse the real block structure of each exam, block by block, and see whether your accuracy holds across the day.

  • Every finished block saved immediately
  • Front-half vs back-half stamina read
  • Recovery day scheduled after
05

Know where you stand

Replaces a spreadsheet and a lot of guessing.

Score Matrix

Grades

Weighted grades that understand how your courses are actually marked — dropped quizzes included — and the score you need on everything left.

  • Target projection for every tier
  • Automatic lowest-score drops
  • Feeds Board Ops as school-exam evidence

Productivity Score

Fair by design

Measured against your own average, never someone else's. Components you don't use are left out, not scored as zero.

  • Plan completion, focus, study activity, consistency
  • 30-day trend on every day badge
  • Past days are frozen, never rewritten

Stats & heatmap

Evidence

Study time, Anki history, questions answered, task completion and a year-long heatmap — the evidence for whether the plan is working.

  • By day, week and month
  • Anki card history
  • Streaks

Block view

Where the day went

Your calendar and your Focus sessions on one timeline, opening on the current hour, so the gap between the plan and the day is visible.

  • Sessions drawn where they actually ran
  • Pomodoro breaks shown as gaps
  • Day and week completion at a glance
06

Keep going

What matters on week nine of a hard block.

GrydRank

Momentum

100 named levels across ten tiers and 51 badges, all earned from work you were doing anyway.

  • XP from tasks, focus, Anki, quizzes and cases
  • Weekly challenges and streaks
  • Selectable taglines

Profiles

New
Your record

A bio, specialty interests, lifetime totals, the last 30 days and personal bests — with a level ring that fills as you climb.

  • Bio and up to three specialty interests
  • Anonymous mode hides your name and bio
  • Shared groups at a glance

Groups & leaderboards

Accountability

An opt-in cohort board, or private groups with their own boards for the people who'll notice when you go quiet.

  • Today, week, month and all-time
  • Invite by name
  • Password-protected groups

Peer Board

Community

A struggle board for your class. Post anonymously or by name, upvote what you recognize, answer what you've been through.

  • Truly anonymous posting
  • Threaded replies
  • XP for helping classmates
07

Research & platform

Everything else medical school quietly expects you to organize.

Research Hub

Collaborate

Ideas, projects and deadlines on one board. Import literature from Google Sheets, share the project, and split the tasks.

  • Seven-stage pipeline to published
  • Screening questions and exclusion reasons
  • Deadline countdowns

Install anywhere

Platform

Installs to your home screen on iPhone and Android with a layout built for a phone, and runs in any laptop browser.

  • Dark, light or sunrise-to-sunset themes
  • Any accent color
  • A ten-step setup with a live preview

See it move.

The homepage has working miniatures of the planner, Quiz Mastery, CaseFlow, Board Ops and more.
