Part I Data Processing Agreement

This DPA governs how MedGryd processes personal data on behalf of the Institution and identifies all subprocessors.

1. Definitions

Controller means the Institution, which determines the purposes and means of processing personal data related to enrolled students.

Processor means MedGryd LLC ("MedGryd"), which processes personal data on behalf of the Institution.

Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

Subprocessor means any third party engaged by MedGryd to process personal data.

Education Records has the meaning assigned by FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g).

2. Scope and Purpose

MedGryd will process Personal Data only as necessary to provide the Service as described in the institutional license agreement. Categories of data processed include:

  • Account identifiers — email address, display name, profile photo URL, Firebase UID
  • Study activity data — tasks, study time, Anki review counts
  • Self-assessed competency data — Skills Vault confidence ratings and notes
  • Assessment data entered by the student — grades, exam scores, projections
  • Quiz performance data — topic mastery scores, attempt history
  • Peer Board posts — stored without display names; UID-linked for moderation only
  • GrydRank data — XP, level, leaderboard stats (opt-in; anonymity toggle available)
  • Presence data — online/offline status, last-seen timestamp

MedGryd will not process Personal Data for any purpose other than providing the Service.

3. Processing Instructions

MedGryd will process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Institution or in accordance with applicable law. The primary instruction is: process data to provide MedGryd's features to enrolled students as described in the Service description.

5. Security Measures

MedGryd implements the following technical and organizational security measures:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS (HTTPS enforced)
  • Firebase Firestore data is encrypted at rest using Google's infrastructure
  • Access to individual student data is restricted by Firestore Security Rules tied to each student's Firebase UID — no student can access another's data
  • Session replays via Sentry are accessible only to the MedGryd development team
  • Activation codes are validated server-side and not retained after validation
  • No plaintext passwords are stored — authentication is fully delegated to Google
  • Nightly encrypted database backups are maintained for disaster recovery, stored in a private, access-restricted repository, and retained for up to 30 days

Sentry session replay note: Approximately 10% of sessions are recorded as interactive replays; 100% of sessions in which a JavaScript error occurs are recorded. Current configuration masks neither text input nor on-screen images — replays and error reports may include on-screen text content entered by the user, anything visible on screen, and default personal data (IP address, account identifiers) sent by the SDK. Replays are used solely for bug diagnosis by the development team and are subject to Sentry's 90-day retention policy.

6. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach affecting Institutional data, MedGryd will:

  • Notify the Institution within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach
  • Provide available details: nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of records affected, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed
  • Cooperate fully with the Institution's incident response

7. Data Subject Rights

MedGryd will assist the Institution in fulfilling data subject rights requests (access, correction, deletion, portability) within 30 days of a documented request from the Institution. Individual students may also submit requests directly to hello@medgryd.com per our Privacy Policy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Upon termination of the institutional license, MedGryd will delete or return all Institutional data within 60 days, as directed by the Institution in writing. Sentry error reports are subject to Sentry's standard 90-day retention policy. Anonymized aggregate statistics with no personally identifiable information may be retained indefinitely for product improvement. MedGryd also maintains nightly database backups on a rolling 30-day cycle for disaster recovery; backups are purged automatically 30 days after creation, independent of and in addition to the deletion timeframe above.

9. Audit Rights

The Institution may request a written summary of MedGryd's security practices and subprocessor agreements once per calendar year. MedGryd will respond within 30 business days. On-site audits may be requested with reasonable advance notice and are subject to mutual scheduling agreement.

Part II FERPA Addendum

This addendum designates MedGryd as a school official under FERPA for institutional licensees and defines the scope of that designation.

10. FERPA Designation

MedGryd is designated as a "school official" of the Institution with a "legitimate educational interest" in accessing education records, as those terms are defined under FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g) and its implementing regulations (34 C.F.R. Part 99), for the purpose of providing the Service.

This designation is limited to:

  • Displaying and storing data the student themselves enters into MedGryd
  • Providing the student with features described in the Service (planner, grade tracker, skills tracker, Quiz Mastery, etc.)
  • Generating aggregate, non-identifiable usage statistics for the Institution for administrative and program improvement purposes

11. Permitted Uses of Education Records

As a designated school official, MedGryd may use education records only to:

  • Provide the contracted Service to enrolled students
  • Diagnose and fix technical errors affecting the Service
  • Generate aggregate, non-identifiable reports for the Institution

12. Prohibited Uses

MedGryd will NOT:

  • Share individually identifiable education records with third parties except as required to operate the Service (as listed in the subprocessor table in Section 4)
  • Use education records for advertising or commercial profiling of any kind
  • Sell education records or any derivative thereof
  • Re-disclose education records in violation of FERPA

13. Annual Notification

The Institution agrees to include MedGryd in its FERPA annual notification to students as a service provider designated as a school official with a legitimate educational interest, consistent with 34 C.F.R. § 99.7.

14. Student Rights Under FERPA

MedGryd acknowledges that students retain all rights under FERPA, including the right to inspect, review, and request amendment of their education records. MedGryd will cooperate with the Institution in honoring these rights and will not impede a student's ability to exercise them.

15. Breach of FERPA Addendum

If MedGryd materially violates this FERPA Addendum, the Institution may terminate the institutional license agreement immediately upon written notice. MedGryd will return or destroy all education records as specified in Section 8 within 30 days of termination.

Execution

This DPA and FERPA Addendum becomes effective upon execution of the institutional license agreement, which incorporates this document by reference. A fully executed copy with both signatures will be provided to the Institution upon request.
