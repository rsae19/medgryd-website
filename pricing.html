— Questions

Asked often.

Is it really free?
Basic is free during the beta and includes the planner, Quiz Mastery, CaseFlow, Board Ops, GrydGuide and deeper explanations. GrydAI chat is included with Plus and Max, which also offer larger weekly AI question-generation allowances.
What are AI tokens for?
Writing new questions from your own lecture slides or Anki decks inside Quiz Mastery. The planner, CaseFlow, Board Ops, GrydGuide, deeper explanations and importing question banks work on every plan. GrydAI chat is a Plus and Max feature with its own daily message allowance.
Which AI models write the questions?
Board-style vignettes are written by Claude Opus 5.5 and course-style questions by Claude Sonnet 5.5. Both are constrained to what your source material actually teaches.
Does it work at my school?
Yes. MedGryd reads Canvas, D2L Brightspace or any iCal feed, and Google Calendar. Cohort extras like shared exams and quiz passwords are set up per school.
Is my data private?
Your data is isolated to your account by database security rules. Leaderboards are opt-in, with an anonymous mode, and board-prep progress is never ranked against anyone. See the Privacy Policy.
How does school licensing work?
We work directly with your program to load its exams, calendars and curriculum for each cohort, then onboard students together. Start the conversation.
