For schools

One platform for a whole cohort.

Your exams, your calendars and your curriculum loaded once — and every student gets the planner, question engine, patient simulator and board plan that go with them. Built with, and piloted by, the DO29 and DO30 classes at Noorda College of Osteopathic Medicine.

01 — Set up once

The school's calendar, in every student's week.

Load a cohort's exams one time and they appear on every student's Exams card, counting down in the same urgency colours. Run an exam in two sittings? Students pick theirs, and the rest stop counting down.

  • Cohort-wide exams that no student can accidentally delete.
  • Alternate sittings declared once, chosen by each student.
  • Daily and weekly quiz passwords delivered onto the task itself.
  • Canvas, D2L Brightspace or any iCal feed, read automatically.

03 — Privacy & security

Student data stays the student's.

Built with FERPA in mind, with a Data Processing Agreement ready for your counsel to review.

  • Per-user isolation enforced by database security rules, not just the app.
  • Shared spaces are member-scoped: a shared course, event or project is readable only by the people in it.
  • AI requests carry the question, never the student: no names or accounts are sent to model providers.
  • No peer comparison on board progress — ever. Board-prep data is never ranked.
  • Opt-in only: leaderboards are opt-in, with a fully anonymous mode.
  • A signed DPA and FERPA terms for every licensing school — read them here.
04 — How it works

From first call to a live cohort.

  1. Talk

    A short call about your curriculum, your LMS and what your students struggle with most.

  2. Configure

    We load your exams, block schedule, quiz passwords and course presets for each cohort.

  3. Onboard

    Students activate and finish a ten-minute setup that pulls in their calendars and coursework.

  4. Support

    Priority support from the people who built it — medical students who know your calendar.
