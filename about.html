Our story

Built in the trenches of medical school.

A real problem, and a solution built by the people who live it — between lectures, and tested before exams.

01

The problem

As a first-year osteopathic student at Noorda College of Osteopathic Medicine, Raayed Saeed was drowning in disconnected tools — a calendar here, flashcards there, a spreadsheet for grades, another for research. Nothing talked to anything else.

02

The decision

So he built something that did. It started as a weekly planner, wired to the school's own calendar.

03

The build

Classmates started using it. Aaron Smith built Quiz Mastery; together they added a patient simulator, a board plan and everything around them. It became a study operating system.

04

The standard

Every feature was born from a real pain point and tested in real coursework. MedGryd isn't designed in a vacuum — it's designed in the library at 11pm before a shelf exam.

— Mission

Give every medical student one powerful platform for the chaos of their academic life — so they can focus on becoming great physicians.
